# Indexa

A beautiful, minimalistic bookmark manager with Chrome import, smart tagging, and a stunning liquid glass UI.

🔗 **[Try it live on CodePen](https://codepen.io/krunalhp/full/VYjPqVe)**

---

## ✨ Features

- **Import Chrome Bookmarks** — Seamlessly import your existing bookmarks from Chrome's HTML export
- **Smart Auto-Tagging** — Automatically categorize bookmarks based on their URLs
- **Tag Management** — Create custom tags with colors to organize your bookmarks
- **Bulk Operations** — Select multiple bookmarks and tag them all at once
- **Search** — Quickly find bookmarks by title or URL
- **Export & Import** — Backup and restore your bookmark data anytime

---

## 🔒 Your Privacy Matters

**Your data never leaves your device.**

All your bookmarks are stored **locally in your browser's storage** (localStorage). This app does not:
- Upload your data to any server
- Track your browsing habits
- Share any information with third parties

Everything stays on your computer. If you want to permanently delete your data, simply clear your browser's website data for this app, and it's gone forever.

---

## 💻 Run Locally

Just open `index.html` in your browser — no server required!
